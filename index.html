Public Transit Tips

  • Obilet App: Download the "Obilet" app to check schedules and buy tickets for major intercity buses (Otobüs).
  • Dolmuş (Minibuses): For shorter distances (like Mardin to Amed, or Van to the Kapıköy border), Dolmuş vehicles are faster and depart when full. Pay cash directly to the driver.
  • Otogar: Intercity buses leave from the central bus terminal, always called "Otogar". You usually need a local city minibus to reach the Otogar from the city center.
  • Cash is King: Always carry smaller Turkish Lira notes (50, 100, 200) for Dolmuş fares and quick snacks at rest stops. Transit inflation fluctuates, so keep ample cash on hand.
  • The Maraş-Tatvan Gap: There are rarely direct buses from Maraş to Tatvan. You will likely need to buy a ticket to Diyarbakır or Batman, and immediately catch a connecting bus to Tatvan.
